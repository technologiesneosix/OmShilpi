A Legacy of Artisanship

For nearly three decades, Om Shilpi Jewels has translated the royal heritage of Rajasthan into timeless adornments, where every piece tells a story of meticulous craft.

The Heritage Flagship

Rooted in the Pink City of Jaipur, our design language is inextricably linked to Rajasthan's illustrious history of royal jewelry. The architecture of our city—its intricate jali work, grand courtyards, and vibrant frescoes—informs the geometry and soul of our creations.

We operate from a restored haveli that serves as both our creative sanctuary and artisan workshop. Here, ancient techniques like Meenakari (enameling) and Kundan (gem setting) are practiced with the same reverence as they were centuries ago, seamlessly blended with contemporary sensibilities.
